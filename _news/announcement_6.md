Congratulations to Meri Okorie on the publication of the first two papers from her PhD! In *Alzheimer’s & Dementia*, Meri and colleagues show how [cross-ancestry polygenic risk scores can improve Alzheimer’s disease risk prediction in multiethnic cohorts](https://doi.org/10.1002/alz.71529) and how [dementia polyexposure scores relate to Alzheimer’s disease endophenotypes in a diverse population](https://doi.org/10.1002/alz.71567).
