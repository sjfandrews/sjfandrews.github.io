Thrilled to receive an Alzheimer’s Association Research Grant Award (AARGA) for "Gene-Environment Interactions for Personalized Dementia Risk Assessment"! This project will examine how Alzheimer’s disease polygenic and clinical risk scores interact to influence biomarkers of disease pathology and clinical progression.
