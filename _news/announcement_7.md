Our new review, led by Paulina Tolosa-Tort, is now published in *npj Dementia*! ["Toward personalized medicine in AD/ADRD through genetic-exposome dementia risk assessments"](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44400-026-00087-7) outlines how integrating genetic and environmental risk factors could support earlier, more personalized, and more equitable dementia prevention.
